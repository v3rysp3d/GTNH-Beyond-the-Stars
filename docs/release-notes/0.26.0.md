# GTNH: Beyond the Stars 0.26.0

A gameplay release on top of 0.25.2, for **GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2**. No structure changes: existing machines keep standing
and running. Some Eye numbers change (below), so read the Gameplay and Removed inputs sections before you update a
world with a T7 or T8 Eye. As always, **back up your world first**. [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md) has the full guide.

## Gameplay

- **Every Eye tier makes Raw Star Matter.** From T2 up, a lit Eye makes Raw Star Matter (Condensed Raw Stellar Plasma
  Mixture) every Eye-minute: **N x 50,000 L** x parallel x mean Y x r at tier N, so 100,000 L at T2 and 500,000 L at
  T10. T5 and T10 earn it every minute, not only when their batch ends. Planets mode earns a quarter; companions and
  transitions earn none. T1 is unchanged: Sol's product already is Raw Star Matter. Sirius A's own 25,000 L Raw Star
  Matter upkeep is now covered four times over at Y 1. The GUI's Raw Star Matter row, the scanner, NEI's star pages
  and the star chart tooltips show it.
- **The galaxy tiers run on GRB stability alone.** Triangulum (T7) and the Milky Way (T8) take no fluid at all; the
  bursts on the GRB Receptor Ports hold them.
  - **Triangulum:** no Molten Magnetar Matter upkeep. It still makes 600 L Ergomatter x Y x r x min(β, 1.5) a cycle.
  - **The Milky Way:** no Ergomatter feed. Its tidal shear Λ drifts at a fixed 2.0 a second (the old default feed's
    drift; halo bursts hold it as before). It makes **300 L Superdense Ergomatter a cycle** x parallel x Y x r, the
    old default output, and a caught satellite crossing pays 300 L x r x parallel.
  - **The Galactic Merger** has no Ergomatter charge: bursts alone hold it while the demand ramps from 1.5 to 6 Bu/t
    (1.5 x 10^13 to 6 x 10^13 EU/t). A failure loses Triangulum only.
  - At T9, the galaxy companions (Triangulum, the Milky Way) have no upkeep either.
- Unchanged: the Magnetar Collapse charge (White and Black Dwarf Matter), The Infall's Superdense Ergomatter charge,
  Inflation's and every universe's Attractor Dark Matter charge, the T9 pellet beam, T6's Antipolarization Agent.

## Universe

- **Each finished universe yields what it holds**, beside its Molten Universium, x parallel x mean Y x r: Excited
  Stellar Plasma 300,000 L, Raw Hyperdense Stellar Plasma 75,000 L, White Dwarf Matter 18,420 L, Black Dwarf Matter
  4,608 L, Magnetar Matter 1,800 L, Ergomatter 72,000 L and Superdense Ergomatter 36,000 L.
- **And every planet**, once a universe: all 43 planet recipes (the Deep Dark included) at parallel 2^(10 - k), each
  parallel succeeding at (1 - 0.05 R) x the universe's field factor, as planets mode rolls them. No H/He and no EU.
  Items go to the ME output bus, fluids to the output hatches.
- **The filter.** The slot bus is a whitelist and is never used up. Empty, everything comes. Planet items keep only
  those planets; star charts of T2 to T8 keep only their matters. Sol's chart does nothing (Raw Star Matter always
  comes). An ME interface or a conveyor can set it.
- **Two toggles** on the Galaxy panel at T10, "Star matter yield" and "Planet yield", switch each part off. They are
  saved with the Eye, also when you wrench it. OpenComputers: `getUniverseYield()`, `setUniverseYield(stars,
  planets)`. Redstone: a Directive Hatch on the new control set **Eye: control the universe's yield** reads the
  strength as a code (bit 1 star matter on, bit 2 planets on; 4 holds both off; 0 leaves the toggles to the GUI). The
  GUI's Yield row says what the next universe gives, and the Galaxy panel says when a Directive Hatch holds it.
- A collapsed universe (a Big Rip or Big Crunch) yields none of this; its Universium salvage is as before.
- NEI: the Universe page lists the star matter, and new pages list every planet's yield.

## Removed inputs

- The Milky Way's Ergomatter feed and its GUI stepper (Galaxy panel). OpenComputers `setFeed8` is kept so scripts do
  not break, but it now returns `false, "Milky Way has no feed since 0.26.0"`.
- Triangulum's Molten Magnetar Matter upkeep, the Galactic Merger's 9,000 L Ergomatter charge, and the T9 galaxy
  companions' Magnetar Matter and Ergomatter upkeep. The Eye no longer pulls Ergomatter or Molten Magnetar Matter
  from its sockets.

## Old worlds

- **Ergomatter and Magnetar Matter already in an Eye's buffers are given back, not lost.** On the first load with
  0.26.0 they move to the Eye's owed outputs (the GUI lists them) and are paid to its output hatches as soon as the
  Eye stands formed. Make sure the output hatches have room (or an ME network behind them): what they cannot hold is
  voided, as for every Eye output. The log says how much was moved.
- A T8 Eye's old feed setpoint is ignored. A lit T7 or T8 keeps running; it simply stops drawing the fluid.
- Throat fuel (Ergomatter on the Conduit) is unchanged: that is the Conduit's own input, not the Eye's.

## Showcase worlds

Two worlds come with this release, as downloads next to the jar. Each holds every Singularity Ladder line at its top
tier and the Eye of Harmony at every tier, T1 to T10, all running with nobody at the controls. Walk or fly east along
the avenue from spawn; the signs there list what stands where and how to teleport. Install them as described in the
README inside each zip (singleplayer: unzip into `saves/`; a server: use the folder as `world`). They need GTNH
2.9.0-RC-2 with this jar.

- **The automation world** runs on real in-world blocks you can open and copy: GT debug hatches and debug fluid tanks
  as endless supplies, Directive Hatches driving trim valves (a Machine Controller and a Fluid Regulator cover on each
  tank), Stellar Field Sensor Hatches steering the conduit Junctions over GT wireless redstone, a real quasar and
  Wormhole Mouths feeding every galaxy-tier Eye and the Homogeny, and one wireless-network top-up for the Eye's EU.
  The only thing the mod does for it is keep the exhibits' chunks loaded (Forge's default limits cannot be covered by
  the pack's chunk loaders).
- **The code world** runs on showcase mode: the mod feeds and holds every machine itself (below). Nothing in it needs
  wiring, so nothing in it breaks if you poke at it.

Break what you like in either: download the zip again for a fresh copy.

## Showcase mode (`/btsshowcase`, test command)

- **`/btsshowcase on|off x y z`** and **`/btsshowcase all on|off [radius]`** switch showcase mode on a Beyond the
  Stars controller (op only; gone with `enableTestCommands=false`, though a flag already set keeps working because it
  is saved in the machine). A showcase machine keeps its supplies and power topped up, holds its star, hole or disc
  stable, switches itself back on after a structure or maintenance stop, voids what its outputs cannot take, and keeps
  its whole footprint loaded, also after a restart. Its GUI and scanner say SHOWCASE MODE. It is a cheat for showcase
  worlds and screenshots, not for survival.
- **`/btsshowcase keep on|off x y z`** (and `all keep`) only keeps the machine's footprint loaded.
- **`/btsshowcase autorig x y z`** builds the automation world's in-world supplies and valves for that machine.
- **`/btsshowcase status`**, **`info x y z`** (any GT machine's scanner lines) and **`fluid x y z <fluid>`** (fill an
  empty input hatch) help check an exhibit.
- The mod now holds one chunk-loading callback for all its tickets. Showcase tickets force each chunk once, however
  many footprints cover it, so a whole showcase world fits in about 140 of Forge's default 200 tickets. The bench
  commands' `keep` tickets are released after a restart, as before.
