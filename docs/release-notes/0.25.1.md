# GTNH: Beyond the Stars 0.25.1

A fix release on top of 0.25.0, for **GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2**. It is safe to drop in over 0.25.0: no structure changes, and
existing machines keep running. As always, **back up your world first**. [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md) has the full guide.

## Building

- **Survival auto-place no longer stops silently at an unloaded chunk.** A big footprint (an Eye T9 is 196 x 218
  blocks) often reaches past your view distance. Auto-place used to stop there and still say "finished", so the Eye
  formed bands short. It now pauses and names the chunk; load it and use the projector again. This covers the Eye,
  Kerr, Quasar Engine, lattice, Homogeny, crowns, Reality machines, conduit elements and the nuclear plant. A machine
  that would reach below y 0 or above y 255 says so.
- **Crafting Input Proxies** are refused in the Eye's item sockets, like Crafting Input Buffers already were.
- **Structure errors use real names.** "hatch_type_gas" is now "Gas Feed Hatch (any input hatch)", and so on for
  every socket.
- **The Kerr's bolt buses refuse ME stocking buses** by name: the governor cannot drain one.

## Gameplay

- **Kerr governor:** it now holds at least the mass its rails and lightest bolts can sustain (8 SM for Neutronium on
  Mk I rails), so a low setpoint no longer lets the hole evaporate. A setpoint of exactly 2 SM still asks for a
  collapse. The GUI shows the floor.
- **Galactic Merger:** charges its 5 L/s of Ergomatter only during the 1,800 s event, so it takes the intended
  9,000 L instead of up to 10,500 L.
- **Galaxy / Planets toggle** asks for a second click while a field is lit.
- **Kerr T1:** Density recipes no longer block GT's compressor recipes before the Density Upgrade.
- **Crown:** a "Source shedding: makes nothing" row while a lattice quasar sheds, and no extra second of EU after a
  jet stops.
- **GRB Receptor Port:** its lifetime total survives a restart, and reading it (GUI, scanner, OpenComputers) no longer
  changes it.
- **Reality Refab:** the first tick after a stall is no longer free.
- Clearer status lines: "KEE not loaded" after a restart, the Field panel says when a Directive Hatch holds the time
  dial or feed, fluid names in Eye GUI rows follow your language, and tooltips give casing counts as ranges.

## Renders

- **Calmer instability everywhere.** Size pulses are now at most 4 % of the radius and brightness at most 12 %, only
  at the worst state, as slow smooth waves. Instability shows through boiling, corona, field lines and disc
  turbulence instead. For example, the overfed magnetar went from +-55 % to +-4 % and the Kerr's no-coverage tilt
  from 8 degrees to 4.
- An Eye T9 or T10 that faces east, south or west could be drawn turned 90 or 180 degrees against its blocks until
  its first update, with the Universe's disc tilted off the crown. It now draws the right way round from the first
  frame.
- Spins, boils and field-line beads no longer jump each time their speed changes (the overfed magnetar's beams used
  to jump about once a second).

## Servers

- **`enableTestCommands`** (new, in the `commands` section of `config/beyondthestars.cfg`, default `true`): set it
  to `false` to remove the 14 builder and bench commands. `/btsquests`, the admin parts of `/btsrecipes`,
  `/btskerr migrate` and `/btseye gates|bec` stay.
