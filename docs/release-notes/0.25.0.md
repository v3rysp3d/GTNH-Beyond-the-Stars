# GTNH: Beyond the Stars 0.25.0

The first public release of the **Singularity Ladder**: an addon for **GregTech: New Horizons 2.9.0-RC-2** that
rebuilds the post-UEV endgame as one connected climb. A lab-grown black hole replaces the Black Hole Compressor, a new
Eye of Harmony burns live stars, dead stars and whole galaxies, and a Quasar Engine grows its own black hole and fires
gamma-ray bursts that power the base and hold the bigger machines stable. Every rung is a large, fully automatable
multiblock with its own questbook chapter, and each one makes the matter or the stability the next one needs.

This is a playtest release. **Back up your world before you install it**, and expect balance changes between
releases. The [README](README.md) explains every machine; [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md) is the full install guide.

## What's in it

- **Kerr Ergosphere Engine (UIV).** A lab black hole in a 65-block cage, with a Compressor Module and an Extractor
  Module docked in its arcs that run GT's compressor, neutronium compressor and extractor recipes at big parallels. It
  takes the Black Hole Compressor's place in every recipe that used its controller. The T2 Density Upgrade refines
  Hyperdense Stellar Mixture and, later, the Quasar Seed.
- **Eye of Harmony, T1 to T10, on one controller.** Each tier adds a band around the last. Stars burn live every
  60-second cycle: Sol, Sirius A, Arcturus, a neutron star, Sirius B, the magnetar SGR 1806-20, then the Triangulum
  galaxy and the Milky Way. Tier steps are events you ride (the Magnetar Collapse, the Galactic Merger). TecTech's
  planet recipes run in it too, including a planets mode at T7 and T8.
  - **T9, the Great Attractor:** a beam of Gated Pellets from the Pellet Injector Hatch holds the infall while
    Galactic Flows come down the filaments. It makes **Attractor Dark Matter**, which every MAX part is built from.
  - **T10, the Universe:** one universe per two-hour batch, kept on its Friedmann curve. It makes **Universium**, and
    it is the only source; nothing before T10 needs it.
- **The Homogeny (UMV to MAX).** A mega-mixer in a glass vortex column: Excited Stellar Plasma Mixture at T1,
  Degenerate Stellar Mixture at T2, and the Entanglement Medium at T3.
- **Quasar Engine (UXV).** Feed it gas and it grows a black hole with an accretion disc and two jets. Its gamma-ray
  bursts go to **Burst Thermaliser Crowns** (EU/t onto your wireless network) or through the **Einstein-Rosen
  Conduit**, and quasar links hold Eye T7 and up stable and speed up the Kerr and the Homogeny.
- **Supermassive Lattice.** The Quasar Engine's top upgrade: a cage of 48 Containment Nodes that lets the hole grow
  past 4,096 solar masses, with Overdrive and Unbound modes for endgame power. It feeds the Supermassive Link that
  opens Eye T10.
- **Reality Diffraction and Reality Refab (MAX).** Two machines on TecTech's BEC network that split the Entanglement
  Medium into six Entangled Regions and rebuild them into the Universal Upgrade Core, the tenth compression tier and
  the Observable Universe star chart.
- **MAX parts.** The eight MAX components, the MAX casing and hull and the wired and wireless MAX hatches get recipes
  on Attractor Dark Matter (RC-2 had none).
- **Circuits.** Three new families (Sidereal, Empyrean and Omega), twelve circuits in all, made in stock GT and
  TecTech machines. They fit every "any circuit of the tier" slot from UHV to MAX at far lower cost than Piko, Quantum
  or Planck circuits. No recipe needs them by name.
- **Renders.** The black holes, the quasar, every star, both galaxies, the Great Attractor, the Universe, the Homogeny,
  the Reality machines and the conduit are drawn by our own shaders, with `high`, `medium` and `low` quality and a
  level-of-detail budget.
- **Questbook.** New chapters (Tier 13 - UXV, Tier 14 - MAX, The Endgame) placed after the pack's Tier 12, plus grafts
  into the UIV and UMV chapters. The book installs itself, keeps your progress, and the Eye ticks its own quests.
- **Automation.** Every action can be driven by AE2, redstone and logic gates, OpenComputers or Steve's Factory
  Manager, with Directive Hatches, Stellar Field Sensor Hatches and Burst Control Hatches. See
  [docs/automation.md](docs/automation.md).
- **The Nuclear Plant** (the mod's older IV to UHV layer) is still here, with its own chapter. The Extraction Platform
  and Yellowcake Mill now have GT's void protection, on by default: a full output stops the machine instead of voiding.

## Requirements

- **GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2, exactly** (GregTech 5.09.54.205, StructureLib 1.4.45, GTNewHorizonsCoreMod 2.9.84). It does not
  run on 2.8.x or the 2.9.0 betas; a later RC may load but can fail in a GregTech class.
- **Java:** whatever your RC-2 instance already runs. Tested on Java 8 and on Java 21 with lwjgl3ify.
- **One jar** on the client and on the server: `beyondthestars-0.25.0.jar`, attached below.
- BetterQuesting and OpenComputers are optional and already in the pack.

## Installing and upgrading

1. Close the game (or stop the server) and **back up the world**. The quest progress lives there too.
2. Remove every older `beyondthestars-*.jar` from `mods/` and its subfolders; a second copy crashes at start.
3. Put the new jar in `mods/` and start. The first start takes about a minute longer while the recipe changes run.
4. On a server, every player needs the same jar, and the server's `config/beyondthestars.cfg` should be copied to
   every client. Crafting always follows the server, but NEI shows the client's recipes; if the recipe switches
   differ, the server names each one in chat when you log in. Copy the server's values and restart the client.
5. Check `logs/fml-client-latest.log` (or the server's) for
   `[BTS] Recipe registration complete: ... registered, 0 skipped.`

### Before you add it to an existing world

With the default config the mod changes some of the pack's own content. Each change has a switch in
`config/beyondthestars.cfg`; set it to `false` before the first start to keep the pack's version.

- **A TecTech Eye of Harmony you have already built stops working.** It still forms but finds nothing to run, because
  our Eye takes over its recipes (`replaceTecTechEyeOfHarmony`). Set the switch to `false` to keep it, and finish or
  cancel any running TecTech Eye recipe before the first start either way: a run in progress is lost.
- **The Black Hole Compressor's controller recipe is removed** (`removeBlackHoleCompressorRecipe`). Placed compressors
  keep working; recipes that used its controller as an ingredient now take a Kerr controller.
- **AE2 patterns and data sticks** encoded for any changed recipe no longer match. Re-encode them after the first start
  (NEI shows the new recipes).
- The Nanochip Assembly Complex's Piko, Quantum, Raw Manifold Bud and Planck steps cost more
  (`raiseStockSpecialCircuits`), and TecTech's Reinforced Spatial / Temporal Structure Casing recipes make 32 instead
  of 4.
- 66 of the pack's quests are edited and the emptied Endgame Goals chapter is hidden (`questbookPackEdits`).
  Completed quests stay completed.

Removing the mod later: run `/btsquests uninstall all` first. The steps are in [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md).

## Known issues

- Chunk-load the **whole footprint** of every big machine. While any chunk of an Eye, Kerr or Quasar is unloaded the
  machine is frozen (nothing runs, nothing is lost).
- On a slower graphics card, a Kerr hole seen up close with background lensing can cost more GPU time than we would
  like; use `renderBackgroundLensing=false` or `renderQuality=medium`. Shaderpacks under Angelica have not had a full
  session yet; `renderQuality=low` brings back the simple renders if one breaks.
- **Eye T9:** wire the Pellet Injector's Burst Control Hatch to a V sensor (the starter kit does this). An unwired
  hatch fires at the middle level, and the GUI warns when the beam is starved. **Eye T10:** a new universe waits until
  bursts reach the crown, so it never burns its Dark Matter with nothing to hold it.
- Survival auto-place of the full T9 Eye can report "finished" with a block of the spiral arms band still missing, so
  the Eye forms one band short. Project the hologram to find the gap and place the block by hand. A fix is coming in the next release.
- `renderQuality=low` for Eye T10 has only been checked outside the game so far.
- The Empyrean and Omega wafer engravings take two fluids, so they need a multiblock engraver.
- The Dark Matter Heating Coil works at GT's top coil level (13,501 K), and the multiblock hologram places GT's own
  coils, so place ours by hand.
- A world that ran a development build from before this release logs once that the fluids `btswhiteholematter` and
  `btsentangledwhitehole` are missing and will be removed. They were the White Hole Matter placeholders that
  Attractor Dark Matter replaced, and no survival recipe made them. A container that still holds one comes up empty,
  with no remap; empty them before updating.
- Not yet seen in a long survival world: a full AE2-driven build, OpenComputers Lua in a world, Steve's Factory
  Manager setups, and the renders on slower cards. Reports on any of these are welcome.

The full list is in the README's [Known issues](README.md#known-issues-and-playtest-notes) section.
