The fuel spine is the heavy-outlined row: ore in on the left, fuel bundles out on the right, and nothing skips a box. The Purification Plant sits directly above F-12 because that is the first of the three steps its water grade multiplies — the second water edge drops into F-19 … F-23, where F-20 and F-21a carry the same ladder at bases 8 and 6, and the thin edge running down the right-hand side to the reactor is the same ladder’s much smaller output term. The three lower lanes never touch the fuel spine; they feed the plant itself.

Two effects, one ruling, one file. The yield term lands on the three recipes that make an oxide — F-12 (base 4), F-20 (base 8) and F-21a (base 6) — because in all three the water is literally the reagent, as in UF₆ + 2 H₂O + H₂ → UO₂ + 4 HF. Nine rungs each, twenty-seven recipes. The output term is a sixth × n / 1000 factor on the reactor’s euDemandOf, in the identical shape D-23’s milestone term and D-24’s upgrade term already use.

Why plain water is still enough

Plain water and distilled water are both graded at waterMilli 1000, so the sixth factor is x × 1000 / 1000 == x and a standing plant derives a bit-identical EU figure to 0.23.0. No panel line appears below Grade 1. Distilled is a full rung of the fuel ladder, not an omission — every step other than these three is grade-blind, so a player who never builds a Purification Plant still reaches all four fuel grades, at half rate.

Grade 1 is the identity rung, which is what let F-20 and F-21a grow ladders without re-pricing a standing line: F-20 gives 8 powder per 2,000 L of tails in 18 s where it gave 20 per 5,000 L in 45 s — identical per litre and per second — and F-21a still gives its 6 per 8 oxalate. All three bases are even, so every one of the twenty-seven rungs lands on an integer, and the largest output is 36.

Worst-first is the mechanic. drawFeedwater sweeps the reactor’s hatches worst-first, so a row holding plain water and Grade 8 spends the plain water — a purified feedwater line has to be a dedicated line to pay anything. All three fuel-line steps register their nine recipes best rung first, which is deliberately the opposite, and for the opposite reason: a fuel line piped with Grade 8 should use it.

The shutdown draw reads the same ladder (0.24.0). The 20-minute decay-heat obligation used to know three fluids while the running draw knew eleven, so a plant plumbed for one purified grade and nothing else ran perfectly and then could not pay its decay heat — a guaranteed meltdown handed to the player who followed the tooltip. Both callers now go through one drainFeedwaterLadder.

And how the four new controllers are crafted

All four shipped with no recipe of any kind — outside /bts kit they could not be obtained, which made everything on this page unreachable. Four Assembler entries, each built out of the casing it is actually walled in, on fresh circuits 14–17 (a colliding circuit number would have been dropped silently by RecipeMapBackend.doAdd). They are machine recipes, so they are not counted in the four production lines above.

  • Site Batching Plant — EV, 30 s, circuit 14, no fluid: 8 Solid Steel Casing + 4 Steel Frame Box + 2 Steel Pipe Casing + 2 Conveyor EV + 2 circuit EV. The cheapest of the four, because it is the one you build first in order to build the silo.
  • Cascade Hall — IV, 45 s, circuit 15: 8 Tungstensteel Turbine Casing + 4 TS Pipe Casing + 4 Motor IV + 8 long TungstenSteel rod + 2 plate RhPdPalladium + 4 circuit IV + 1 ingot molten Pt.
  • Fuel Fabrication Hall — IV, 45 s, circuit 16: 8 Clean Stainless Casing + 4 TS Turbine Casing + 4 Robot Arm IV + 4 Conveyor IV + 2 plate RhPdPalladium + 4 circuit IV + 1 ingot molten Pt.
  • PUREX Canyon — IV, 45 s, circuit 17: 8 Radiation Proof Casing + 4 dense Lead plate + 4 Pump IV + 4 medium Stainless pipe + 2 plate RhPdPalladium + 4 circuit IV + 1 ingot molten Pt.

No bootstrap loop: every casing these four need keeps its stock GT recipe, so the Site Batching Plant is reachable before the Site Batching Plant exists. MTE ids are 28113 (Cascade Hall), 28110, 28111 and 28112 — the Hall moved off 28101, which is burned rather than free: tag 0.5.0 shipped a Turbine Hall bound to it.
