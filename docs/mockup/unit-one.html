Every voxel is one placeable block. Cutaway strips plain casing from the front halves; sockets, pillars and the helix stay. One hologram from the main station raises the whole plant — no module controllers, no docking; the towers are optional checked pieces.
There is no automation bay any more. Rods and feedwater go in at the silo face (a24–a26, c17), coolant and propellant at the spiral chamber that burns them, power comes out of the generator's manifold back wall. The chamber and manifold sockets slide back with the silo as the tier grows.
The four modules never move. They stand on fixed platform rectangles at every tier, so the silo can grow from 9 to 17 wide underneath them without a single cell colliding.
